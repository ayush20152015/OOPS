# Apple MacBook Assignment Context

## Topic focus
Apple MacBook

## Assignment brief summary
This is for Marketing Management Assignment 1: Customer Lens.

The assignment asks students to:
- choose one company or product and keep it consistent across all three assignments
- describe the customer clearly and specifically
- explain the job the customer is hiring the product to do
- identify the actual deciding criterion that leads to purchase
- create a customer value scorecard with functional, emotional, and social value

## Company chosen
Apple MacBook

## Best customer segment to use
A focused segment: freelance creative professionals and startup founders who work on the move and value a dependable premium laptop that supports design, communication, coding, and presentation without technical friction.

Named example customer:
Aarav, a freelance UI/UX designer in Bengaluru, works from cafes, client meetings, and home. He needs a laptop that supports design work, calls, presentations, multitasking, and mobility without performance or battery issues.

## Main deciding criterion
The actual deciding criterion is reduced friction in everyday work.

This means the MacBook is chosen because it helps the customer work smoothly, reliably, and professionally, rather than simply because it is a premium brand.

## Job-to-be-done
The MacBook is hired to provide a portable, high-trust professional workstation that supports creative and knowledge work while moving across locations.

## Competing alternatives
- Windows laptops such as Dell XPS or Lenovo ThinkPad
- iPad with keyboard accessory
- cheaper laptops with more compromises in speed, battery, or experience

## Value scorecard focus
Functional: speed, battery, display, reliability, app ecosystem, portability
Emotional: confidence, premium experience, reduced stress, professional identity
Social: professional image, client perception, creativity-tech identity

## Common rubric mistakes to avoid
- describing customers too broadly (for example, “young urban consumers aged 18–35”)
- listing every possible reason someone might buy a product instead of identifying the one actual deciding criterion
- making a scorecard that is positive and never used again in the analysis
- failing to connect the scorecard to the central purchase decision
- using generic brand appeal instead of real customer behavior and work needs

## Final conclusion for the assignment
The Apple MacBook is not chosen simply because it is a premium brand. It is chosen because it reduces friction in professional work and provides a dependable, smooth, portable experience that helps the customer perform better and feel more confident in daily work.

## Recommended assignment direction
Keep the analysis centered on one named customer and one actual deciding criterion: lower friction in everyday work.
